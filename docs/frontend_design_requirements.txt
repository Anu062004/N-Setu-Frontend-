1. PRODUCT THESIS
This is not a lawyer marketplace. The architecture removes weighted ranking, star ratings, win-ratio rewards, public
portfolios, case narratives and client ratings. The three core rails are credential verification, service incentives, and
accountability. fileciteturn5file0L17-L38
The frontend should therefore communicate access to justice, verified professionals, fair allocation, transparent
process and institutional accountability — never popularity or competitive lawyer shopping.

12. CSC / VLE ASSISTED MODE
CSC/VLE is a first-class human actor. The operator acts for a citizen through a recorded delegated session and
consent window; audit records both principals. fileciteturn5file0L77-L79 fileciteturn5file0L450-L463
Recommended persistent banner: ASSISTED SESSION · Acting for [Citizen] · Consent recorded ·

13. PAYMENT UI
Show a transparent quote before work: amount, currency, fee breakdown, expiry and PSP. UI states: Quote ready →
Payment initiated → Awaiting provider confirmation → Paid/settled → Failed → Offline acknowledgement. The
backend does not self-custody funds. fileciteturn5file0L430-L449
Only verified PSP webhook/server-side status can move the payment to a final paid/settled state; a
forged frontend callback must not do it. fileciteturn5file0L703-L713

The Supreme Court of India can work as a strong editorial anchor because the product concerns access to justice.

language implying the Supreme Court operates or endorses the platform unless such a relationship actually exists.

architecture is ideal for prototypes and design exploration. Keep the image subordinate to typography and grid.
